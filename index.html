05 / THE STUDIO

Filmmaking first.
From the idea
to the final master.

Lanius Films is an independent audiovisual studio based in Barcelona, working across film direction, creative production, AI filmmaking and post-production.

Led by filmmaker Jorge Sánchez, the studio brings 10+ years of production and post-production experience to narrative films, self-initiated branded concepts and ongoing cinematic worlds. Each piece is shaped as a complete audiovisual work: story, image, performance, music, voice and sound.

Projects move from concept and visual development through production, editing, colour and sound. Generative tools are part of that process, guided by cinematic direction, continuity and the craft of the final finish.
