About

Lanius Films is an independent filmmaking label focused on cinematic experimentation and generative production.

Our current work explores AI filmmaking, generative characters, horror archives, fashion-led visual storytelling and speculative commercial campaigns.

The goal is not to generate isolated clips, but to build coherent pieces with direction, atmosphere, editing and a recognizable point of view.
